Best Practices for Dimensional Analysis

  • Identify Given & Desired Units First: Always begin by writing down your starting number with its unit on the far left, and note your final target unit on the right.
  • Set Up Diagonal Cancellation: Arrange your conversion factors so that unwanted units appear in opposite positions (numerator vs. denominator) to allow clean cancellation.
  • Multiply Top, Divide Bottom: When evaluating your grid, multiply all numerator values together, then divide by the product of all denominator values.
  • Perform Sanity Checks: Converting from a larger unit to a smaller unit (e.g., meters to centimeters) must produce a larger numerical value.
  • Maintain Proper Units throughout: Never write a number without its unit in your work—units are essential guideposts for verifying calculations.
