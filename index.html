Best Practices for Dimensional Analysis

  • Identify Starting & Desired Units First: Place your starting measurement in the top-left box, and note your target unit on the right.
  • Orient Conversion Factors Diagonally: Arrange conversion factors so unwanted units appear in opposite positions (numerator vs. denominator) for complete cancellation.
  • Multiply Top, Divide Bottom: Calculate the final answer by multiplying all numerators together and dividing by the product of all denominators.
  • Verify Reasonable Scale: Converting from a larger unit to a smaller unit (e.g., feet to centimeters) must result in a larger numerical value.
